Product Introduction

Exploring the craftsmanship, tech-driven structures, and applications of our knitted textiles.

Shanyue Textile integrates advanced German circular knitting machines with premium eco-friendly raw materials to supply sportswear brands, undergarment designers, and functional fashion makers worldwide. Explore our specialized performance fabrics below.

Fabric Engineering Values

Perfect Weight Balance

Engineered densities ensuring durability while preserving light, breathable next-to-skin touch.

Moisture Management

Specialized knit channels draw moisture away from the skin, speeding up evaporation.

Skin Safety & Comfort

All-natural botanical dye extracts and organic cotton blends suited for sensitive skins.

Certified Compliance

OEKO-TEX Standard 100, Global Recycled Standard (GRS) audited supply line.

Functional & Performance Fabrics

Deodorizing Spandex Jersey
消臭氨纶平纹 (单向导湿)

Engineered using a premium viscose carrier embedded with advanced active deodorizing agents, this fabric provides permanent, wash-resistant odor neutralization. The specialized spandex single-jersey knit structure is optimized for high mobility, comfort, and wear resistance.

  • Composition: Deodorizing Viscose / Spandex Blend.
  • Moisture Control: Unidirectional moisture-wicking technology that rapidly forces perspiration to the outer surface, keeping the skin side dry and fresh.
  • Common Uses: High-performance athletic undergarments, sports underwear, daily activewear.
  • Key Advantage: Permanent antibacterial and deodorizing protection combined with fast-drying comfort.

Vitamin E Cotton Jersey
维他命E平纹布 (单向导湿)

A specialized skin-care textile infused with natural Vitamin E oils extracted from rapeseed flowers. Vitamin E is a powerful natural antioxidant that shields cell membranes from free radicals. This fabric offers skin-friendly moisturization, actively nourishing your skin while you wear it.

  • Composition: Natural Cotton blended with Vitamin E functional fibers.
  • Skin Affinity: Mildly acidic pH rating matching the skin's natural pH level, reducing skin irritation.
  • Common Uses: Maternity wear, infant garments, premium sleepwear, nourishing base layers.
  • Key Advantage: Excellent skin moisturization, anti-aging benefits, and high bio-compatibility.

Pearl Fiber Spandex Jersey
珍珠氨纶平纹 (远红外 & 消臭)

Crafted by embedding micronized natural pearl powders into the fibers, this luxury knit boasts an outstanding far-infrared emissivity coefficient of 0.88. Clinically verified testing indicates it raises skin surface temperature by up to 1.59°C in 30 minutes, actively promoting blood circulation.

  • Composition: 20%–30% Pearl functional fibers / Spandex Blend.
  • Micro-circulation: Increases wearer's blood flow volume by 27.8% and skin blood velocity by 23.7%.
  • Common Uses: Thermal wellness garments, high-end base layers, recovery activewear.
  • Key Advantage: Promotes circulation, retains heat, and provides natural deodorization with a smooth, pearl-like sheen.

Hyaluronic Acid Hydration Jersey
玻尿酸氨纶平纹

Nicknamed the "wearable hydrating mask," this fabric incorporates active hyaluronic acid (HA) molecules—one of nature's most hydrophilic molecules. It continuously releases moisture to the skin, helping lock in hydration, boost skin elasticity, and prevent dryness and roughness.

  • Composition: Hyaluronic Acid treated Micro-modal / Spandex.
  • Laundering Durability: Tested to retain substantial skin-moisturizing performance even after 20+ domestic wash cycles.
  • Common Uses: Luxury sleepwear, dry-season base layers, anti-chafing yoga apparel.
  • Key Advantage: Active skin hydration, silk-like drape, and anti-static defense.

Cool-Touch Ice Fabric
（凉霸）冰感面料

Woven from natural cotton linter cellulose fibers, this fabric offers immediate contact cooling. Unlike synthetic cool fabrics containing mineral powders that heat up quickly, our botanical cool-touch technology ensures prolonged heat dissipation, high moisture absorbency, and static control.

  • Composition: Cotton linter derived natural cellulose filaments.
  • Eco-Sustainability: 100% biodegradable in soil and water, returning to the nature cycle without chemical waste.
  • Common Uses: Summer activewear, sports T-shirts, lightweight active jerseys, hot-weather loungewear.
  • Key Advantage: High-performance instant Q-max cooling combined with silky drape and eco-friendly GRS certification.

Graphene Modal Wicking Jersey
石墨烯木代尔氨纶平纹 (面单向导湿)

A high-tech hybrid fabric combining graphene's multi-functional properties with modal's natural softness. Graphene provides advanced low-temperature far-infrared warmth, >99% antibacterial protection, and anti-static control, while the bio-mimetic honeycomb wicking backing forces sweat away from the body.

  • Composition: Graphene Polyester / Modal / Spandex.
  • Visual Customization: Outer surface can be printed with hidden designs that reveal themselves dynamically when the fabric gets wet.
  • Common Uses: Infants apparel, sports activewear, outdoor base layers, maternity clothing.
  • Key Advantage: 99% antibacterial rate, static protection, and dynamic moisture management.
