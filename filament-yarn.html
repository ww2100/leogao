Filament Yarn

Innovative industrial and automotive filament solutions.

We deliver high-performance filament yarns engineered for industrial sewing threads, automotive interiors, and outdoor performance fabrics. Advanced spinning, draw-texturing, and interlacing technology give each filament exceptional strength, dye uniformity, and dimensional stability, ensuring it withstands demanding industrial sewing or lamination processes. Custom deniers, finishes, and package formats help converters move seamlessly from lab approval to mass production without compromising efficiency.
