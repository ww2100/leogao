Dope Dyed Yarn

Eco-friendly color solutions for sustainable textile production.

原液着色技术通过在纺丝阶段加入色母，大幅减少了后道染整的耗水量与化学品排放，是真正的环保色纱。这种工艺不仅节能减排，还能提供更卓越的色牢度和色彩饱和度，是可持续纺织的首选。

Eco-Benefits

  • Zero Water Consumption in Dyeing
  • Superior Color Fastness
  • Reduced Carbon Footprint

Main Products

  • Dope Dyed Polyester DTY
  • Black & Color Nylon Chip
  • Customized Color Masterbatch

Applications

  • Home Furnishing
  • Sports Apparel
  • Sustainable Global Brands
