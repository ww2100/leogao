Dope Dyed Yarn

Eco-friendly color solutions for sustainable textile production.

Dope dyeing injects masterbatch pigments directly into the polymer melt, removing downstream dyeing steps and dramatically reducing water, steam, and chemical usage. The technology delivers deep, saturated shades with outstanding light and rubbing fastness, making it a flagship solution for sustainability-driven mills and brands. By controlling color at the chip level, we guarantee lot-to-lot repeatability, easier inventory planning, and faster launches for seasonal programs or evergreen basics.

Eco-Benefits

  • Zero Water Consumption in Dyeing
  • Superior Color Fastness
  • Reduced Carbon Footprint
