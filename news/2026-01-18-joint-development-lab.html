Partnerships · January 18, 2026

Joint Development Lab Launches in Ho Chi Minh City

Our yarn engineering team now prototypes on-site with two knitting groups, accelerating fit approvals for athleisure and lounge programs.

Rapid co-creation for global collections. The lab aligns yarn design, knitting, and finishing in one room so capsule briefs can move from concept board to wear test in under 10 days.

The new space features a mini creel line, two circular knitting machines, and a finishing corner with a compact stenter. Merchandisers can dial in drape and recovery while watching real-time adjustments to twist levels or lycra plating.

Our Vietnam technical team connects daily with the Shaoxing R&D center via a shared PLM environment. Once a fabric is locked, the digital thread is handed over to production with pre-approved tolerances, reducing redundant pilot runs.

Brands working through the studio gain visibility into local yarn and trim libraries, enabling smarter near-shore sourcing strategies for ASEAN fulfillment. The lab calendar is now open for March concept meetings with focus on lightweight fleece and resort travel knits.

Customers can book slots through their account manager or by writing to gaofei@yarnsvc.com with project details.
