FDY Production Workshop

From polymer chip to finished filament — our FDY line combines melt spinning, high-speed drawing, and precision winding in a single continuous process, delivering yarns of exceptional tenacity and uniformity.

FDY (Fully Drawn Yarn) is a premium category of synthetic filament yarn produced through an integrated one-step process that merges melt spinning with full drawing and heat setting. Unlike the two-step POY-DTY route, FDY technology orients and crystallizes the polymer chains in a single pass, resulting in yarns with superior tensile strength, excellent dimensional stability, and outstanding dye uniformity. Our FDY workshop is equipped with advanced high-speed spinning lines capable of producing polyester and nylon FDY in deniers ranging from 50D to 600D, serving demanding applications in apparel, home textiles, automotive interiors, and industrial fabrics worldwide.

Production Process

Each spool of FDY passes through a tightly controlled sequence — every step is monitored in real time to ensure batch-to-batch consistency.

1
Drying & Feeding
Polymer chips are dried to <30 ppm moisture and fed into the extruder under nitrogen blanket.
→
2
Melt Extrusion
Chips are melted at 285–295 °C and pushed through a spin beam with precision metering pumps.
→
3
Quench & Spin
Filaments exit spinnerets into a cross-flow quench chamber for controlled cooling and solidification.
→
4
Drawing & Setting
Heated godet rolls stretch filaments 3–5× at precise speeds, locking in orientation.
→
5
Interlacing
Air interlacing jets apply knots for filament cohesion, tuned per customer specification.
→
6
Winding
High-speed automatic winders produce uniform packages at 3,500–5,000 m/min.

Equipment & Quality Control

Every machine and test protocol is chosen to eliminate variation — because our customers build their brands on consistency.

Spinning System

  • High-speed FDY spinning lines with closed-loop temperature control
  • Precision spin pumps (±0.5% throughput accuracy)
  • Multi-zone heated godet drawing units
  • Online tension monitoring at each winding position

Quality Lab

  • Denier & filament count verification (ASTM D1907)
  • Tensile testing — tenacity, elongation at break (ASTM D2256)
  • Boiling water shrinkage (ASTM D2259)
  • Oil pickup analysis by solvent extraction
  • Dye-uptake uniformity (visual + spectrophotometer)

Inspection & Packing

  • 100% visual inspection under standardized lighting
  • Package weight verification (±0.5% tolerance)
  • Barcode traceability — lot number → bobbin position
  • Export-grade packaging with moisture barrier
