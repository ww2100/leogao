High-Tech Functional Yarn

Advanced antistatic, thermal bonding, composite, and protective fiber solutions.

We deliver a premium portfolio of high-tech functional yarns engineered for highly specialized environments and protective wear. From carbon-based and metal-oxide conductive fibers that eliminate static electricity, to low-melting thermal bonding yarns and high-tenacity sheath-core composites, our materials are designed to satisfy rigorous safety and mechanical benchmarks. Working directly with top-tier industrial researchers, we customize finishes, cross-sections, and melting ranges to ensure seamless downstream weaving, knitting, and sewing performance.

Conductive & Antistatic

  • Black Conductive Yarn: Carbon-black based polyester/nylon cores for ESD protection in heavy industries.
  • Grey Conductive Yarn: Lighter shading designed for light-colored and white fabrics.
  • Dyeable White Conductive: Oxide-medium based, allowing seamless dyeing without compromising conductivity.
  • Blended Network Yarns: twisted DTY/FDY ready for weaving.

Low-Melting Hot Melt

  • Polyester Hot Melt: Controlled melting points at 110°C, 160°C, and 180°C for exceptional bonding.
  • Nylon Hot Melt: Controlled melting at 100°C and 140°C with high dimensional stability.
  • Applications: 3D shoe uppers, Bonded sewing threads, technical bindings, chenille yarns, and trouser hem adhesives.

Sheath-Core Composites

  • PA/PET Sheath-Core: Polyamide 6 skin for dyeability and wear, Polyester core for high modulus and cost savings.
  • High-Tenacity Composite FDY: Doubles the strength of standard Nylon FDY for heavy sewing.
  • Applications: Care label fabrics, bag construction, tents, tactical outdoor gears, socks, and sports underwear.

Specialty Performance

  • Moisture-Wicking Grooved: Hydrophilic modified capillary grooves (absorbs droplets in <2s vs Coolmax <5s).
  • PP Fine-旦 Antibacterial: Ultra-fine polypropylene with active Ag+ antibacterial and magnetic therapy properties.
  • High-Temp Flame-Retardant PPS: Permanent flame-retardant PPS color yarns for industrial filtration and safety uniforms.
  • Nylon Sea-Island Microfiber: Ultra-soft hand feel for premium suede, synthetic leather, and cleaning cloths.
