About Us

Trusted Asia sourcing partner

With 25 years of profound heritage in the global textile industry, Shaoxing Shanyue Textile Co., Ltd. has established itself as a premier, vertically integrated supplier and international sourcing partner. Strategically connecting global demands with advanced manufacturing hubs, we coordinate a highly optimized production footprint spanning China and Southeast Asia to ensure tariff efficiency, supply stability, and cost-effectiveness for every shipment.

Our competitive edge lies in our comprehensive resource integration and supply chain orchestration. Instead of functioning as a traditional trading broker, Shanyue Textile acts as a vertical hub that consolidates raw materials, advanced manufacturing, and global logistics:

  • Cross-Border Production Footprint: We seamlessly bridge the high-technology filament extrusion and fine-gauge knitting capabilities in China with the tariff-advantaged, cost-efficient garment assembly bases in Southeast Asia (such as Vietnam and Cambodia). This dual-region strategy allows global brands to optimize tax benefits, mitigate supply chain risks, and achieve flexible lead times.
  • Vertical Industrial Integration: We integrate the entire manufacturing lifecycle under a single quality control standard. We coordinate raw material supply—from our state-of-the-art spinning workshop running high-speed winders up to 5,000 m/min—directly with our computerized knitting lines (14G–44G) and finishing partners. By unifying yarn spinning, circular knitting, and dyeing/finishing under one management system, we eliminate quality discrepancies and communication overhead typical of multi-vendor supply chains.
  • Consolidated Warehousing & Smart Logistics: Utilizing our computerized ASRS automatic retrieval warehouse, we aggregate products from diverse production lines. This allows us to provide custom sorting, mixed batch consolidation, and export-ready packaging. Strategically located near major global ports like Ningbo and Shanghai, we handle shipping documentations, custom clearances, and rapid port routing, delivering a seamless 'fiber-to-fabric' service directly to our clients' overseas warehouses.

Our product portfolio reflects this integrated expertise, offering premium polyester filaments (FDY), eco-friendly dope-dyed yarns, high-performance cooling fabrics, and high-density circular knit fabrics tailored for sportswear, fashion, and industrial applications. At Shanyue Textile, we integrate resources so you can focus on your business.

Product Portfolio

Our yarn portfolio is anchored by specialized platforms—Filament, Spun, Dope-Dyed, Functional, and Cooling Yarns—ensuring customers can source every specification from high-speed weaving lots to advanced protective and cooling solutions under one roof.

Filament & Blend Yarn

  • DTY, FDY, and POY programs engineered for circular knitting, warp knitting, and weaving needs.
  • Functional blends featuring cationic polyester, nylon 6/66, and recycled raw materials.
  • Consistency-focused twisting, intermingling, and interlacing options for technical fabrics.

Spun Yarn Solutions

  • Ring-spun, open-end, compact, and vortex constructions for apparel, home, and industrial use.
  • Cotton, polyester, viscose, modal, and Tencel blends covering 6s–80s count ranges.
  • Package-dyed and melange capabilities for fashion-forward texture and color effects.

Dope-Dyed Advantages

  • Color-locked yarns with superior light, wash, and rubbing fastness for outdoor and workwear segments.
  • Significant water and chemical savings versus piece dyeing, supporting sustainability goals.
  • Extensive shade library plus rapid custom development with manageable minimums.

Functional & Specialty

  • Carbon and metal-oxide conductive antistatic yarns for ESD garments and high-end knitwear.
  • Polyester and Nylon thermal bonding hot-melt filaments for 3D shoe uppers and bonded threads.
  • Sheath-core composite yarns, grooved capillary moisture-wicking, and flame-retardant PPS yarns.

PE Cooling Solutions

  • Single-component and bi-component PE/PA cooling yarns providing physical thermal dispersion.
  • Color-spun, moiré, and segment-dyed cooling filaments for premium mattress and sportswear.
  • Eco-friendly biodegradable, recycled, and antibacterial PHMB cool-sensing yarns.
