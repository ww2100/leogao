Spun Yarn

High-quality spun yarns for knitting, weaving, and home textiles.

Our spun yarn program spans premium combed cotton, polyester-rich blends, and viscose-mix counts for both knitting and weaving platforms. The yarns deliver the soft hand and durability required by modern apparel and home textile brands, while tight process control protects evenness, twist balance, and contamination levels on every batch. Dedicated QA teams track moisture, CV%, and strength so every cone arrives production-ready for dyeing, compact weaving, or high-speed circular knitting.
