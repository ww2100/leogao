Product Introduction

Exploring the craftsmanship, tech-driven structures, and applications of our knitted textiles.

Shanyue Textile integrates advanced German circular knitting machines with premium eco-friendly raw materials to supply sportswear brands, undergarment designers, and functional fashion makers worldwide. Explore our specialized performance fabrics below.

Fabric Engineering Values

Perfect Weight Balance

Engineered densities ensuring durability while preserving light, breathable next-to-skin touch.

Moisture Management

Specialized knit channels draw moisture away from the skin, speeding up evaporation.

Skin Safety & Comfort

All-natural botanical dye extracts and organic cotton blends suited for sensitive skins.

Certified Compliance

OEKO-TEX Standard 100, Global Recycled Standard (GRS) audited supply line.
