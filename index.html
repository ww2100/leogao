Factory Showcase

Tour Facility

Smart Material Transport

Overhead Automated Monorail Carrier System

Our manufacturing facility is equipped with state-of-the-art intelligent overhead monorails. This system seamlessly transports newly produced yarn rolls from the spinning floor directly to the packaging unit, minimizing manual contact and ensuring maximum product purity and logistics efficiency.

Core Spinning Technology

High-Performance Melt Spinning Extrusion Line

At the heart of our production is a heavy-duty melt spinning and extrusion system capable of processing a wide range of polymer chips. This advanced line enables precise control over filament denier, tenacity, and elongation, ensuring consistent yarn quality across every production run.

Advanced Manufacturing

Precision Bobbin Winding & Texturing Lines

High-performance winding stations operate around the clock to process premium filaments. This step ensures consistent tension control, structural integrity, and uniform bobbin winding for subsequent textile applications.

Quality Control

State-of-the-Art Testing & Quality Inspection Center

Our in-house laboratory is equipped with precise analytical instruments to verify physical properties such as denier consistency, tensile strength, and dyeing uniformity, guaranteeing that every batch conforms to international standards.

Logistics & Storage

Spacious & Organized Product Warehousing

Finished yarn products are systematically organized and stored on robust rack systems in our clean warehouse. This optimized layout facilitates efficient inventory management and quick dispatching to global clients.
