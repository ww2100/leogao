PE Cool-Sensing & Cooling Yarn

Premium Polyethylene filaments and blends delivering direct physical cooling comfort.

We deliver advanced PE (Polyethylene) cool-sensing yarns designed to offer outstanding thermal conductivity and an immediate cooling touch. Spanning single-component PE filaments, bi-component PE/PA composites, and dope-dyed color selections, our cooling portfolio provides zero-water coloring, superior color fastness, and micro-elasticity. Backed by GRS, OEKO-TEX, and Intertek certifications, our eco-friendly cooling solutions meet the high standards of global activewear, bedding, and home textile brands.

Filaments & Composites

  • Single-Component PE: 100% polyethylene filaments providing absolute, physics-based thermal dispersion. Available in bright, semi-dull, full-dull, and profiled shapes.
  • Bi-Component PE/PA: Blends Polyethylene with Polyamide (Nylon) for a balanced ratio. Delivers micro-elasticity, excellent wear resistance, and high fabric strength.

Color-Spun & Textures

  • Dope-Dyed PE Cooling: Masterbatch color is injected directly during extrusion. Over 60 catalog colors available with low carbon footprint and zero wash-off.
  • Moiré Cooling Filament: A structural PE/PA blend producing smooth, flat fabrics with custom cloud-like moiré effects and high cooling values.
  • Segment-Dyed (Space-Dyed): Deep background stripes and light-on-dark styles (75D to 210D) for stylish activewear.

Eco-Friendly & Functional

  • Biodegradable PE: Bio-degradable PE DTY/FDY that breaks down into CO2 and H2O in soil/water (Intertek certified).
  • Recycled PE: GRS-certified recycled polyethylene cooling filaments from post-consumer sources.
  • Antibacterial PE: Uses non-leaching PHMB technology to deliver a 99.9% bacteria reduction rate.
  • Dyeable & Wear-Resistant: Customized polymers for heavy-use cooling fabrics.

Staple Fiber & Fillings

  • PE Air-Conditioner Fiber: High-coolness short staple fiber designed for needle-punched non-woven substrates and loose filling.
  • Specifications: 8D * 64mm staple length.
  • Applications: Summer mattresses, cool sleeping pillows, mattress quilting, and lightweight summer quilts.
